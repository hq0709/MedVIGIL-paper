\begin{abstract}
Medical vision--language models are evaluated on intact image--question
pairs, but safe clinical use requires more: a model must recognise when the
evidential basis for an answer has failed. We formalise this as an
\emph{evidence contract} and measure its violation as \emph{silent failure},
a fluent non-refusal answer under perturbed evidence. A 300-case suite
authored by four board-certified radiologists establishes the phenomenon in
2D across sixteen models: capability and safe failure diverge, silent failure
rises with clinical risk tier, and a construction-blind radiologist leads the
composite score by 14.1 points. Annotated labels bound the suite at a few hundred items and leave
pretraining overlap impossible to exclude from within. We remove both constraints by
\emph{computing} probe answers instead of annotating them: whether a lesion
would contact a critical structure after isotropic growth is decided exactly
by one distance transform over an expert lesion mask and an automatic
anatomy segmentation. This yields 9{,}484 verifiable probes over 588 CT volumes at zero
annotation cost, querying states that never occurred. The silent-failure
finding replicates and worsens, dataset recognition measures 0.0--5.0\%
against a live positive control, and the failure localises: models perceive the annotation and
execute the underlying comparison exactly, but cannot estimate distance from
the image. Two metrics validated in 2D do not survive the change of modality; we
report the corrections. The suite, harness and cached
outputs are released.
\end{abstract}

\IEEEPARstart{M}{edical} vision--language models (VLMs) have moved from
research prototypes into clinical imaging workflows, open and proprietary
alike~\cite{li2023llavamed,chen2024huatuogptvision,openai2024gpt4o,anthropic2024claude3,gemini2023technical},
in tasks from report assistance to decision support~\cite{moor2023medflamingo,wu2023radfm}.
As they approach deployment, accuracy on intact inputs stops being a
sufficient description of behaviour: a model must also behave safely when the
answer-relevant region is obscured, when the question embeds a premise the
image contradicts, or when the requested quantity is not recoverable from
what was acquired.

There is growing evidence that current systems do not. Frontier VLMs reach
top rank on medical benchmarks without receiving an
image~\cite{asadi2026mirageillusionvisualunderstanding} and underperform
young children on tasks requiring genuine perception~\cite{chen2026babyvision}.
Medicine is where this is hardest to detect: the literature is rich enough
that a plausible answer can be assembled from question templates
alone~\cite{goyal2017vqa,agarwal2020causalvqa}, so a fabricated answer reads
like a grounded one and leaves no trace in an accuracy score.

Existing evaluation is not built to see it. Medical VQA
datasets~\cite{lau2018vqarad,liu2021slake,zhang2023pmcvqa} and the systems
trained on them~\cite{li2023llavamed,moor2023medflamingo,wu2023radfm} presume
valid inputs, as does a concurrent audit of grounding
pathologies~\cite{chen2026auditfrontier}. Hallucination benchmarks~\cite{rohrbach2018chair,li2023pope,chen2024medihallv1,chang2025medheval,gu2024medvh}
score unsupported content under otherwise valid inputs, HEAL-MedVQA tests
grounding through localisation~\cite{nguyen2025healmedicine}, and selective
prediction~\cite{geifman2017selective,ren2023selfevaluation,wen2024knowyourlimits},
abstention benchmarks~\cite{kirichenko2025abstentionbench,madhusudhan2026mmaqa,moratelli2026deflectionbench,li2025jba}
and risk-stratified evaluation~\cite{wang2025csedb,singhal2023medpalm} treat
refusal decision-theoretically. None targets the case in which preserving an
answer despite broken visual evidence is itself the unsafe act.

We formalise that case as an \emph{evidence contract}. While the
answer-relevant evidence is intact, the correct behaviour is to answer; once
it has been removed, corrupted or contradicted, the correct behaviour is to
recognise that the basis has failed. \emph{Silent failure under perturbed
evidence}, a non-refusal answer that accepts the perturbation
(Fig.~\ref{fig:motivation}), is the measurable failure mode.

The conference version of this work~\cite{jiang2026medvigil} operationalises
the contract in 2D with a 300-case suite supervised by four
board-certified radiologists: two annotate every case in parallel, a senior
radiologist consolidates the manifest, and a fourth, from a separate
institution and blinded to construction, answers every probe as a human
reference. Auditing sixteen models over 2{,}556 probes showed
that capability and trustworthy behaviour do not reduce to one ranking, that
silent failure rises with clinical risk tier, and that the blinded
radiologist leads the composite by 14.1 points.

Two objections cannot be settled from inside that result, and the conference
version records both as open. \emph{Scale}: 300 cases is what four clinicians
can author, and a reader may ask whether the finding survives an order of
magnitude. \emph{Pretraining overlap}: the suite draws on VQA-RAD, SLAKE, ROCO
and public radiograph collections that almost certainly intersect the
pretraining mixtures of every proprietary model audited; the conference
version argues that the perturbations postdate those corpora, which is sound
but is an argument rather than a measurement. Both share a root cause: the
labels are \emph{annotated}, so probes are expensive and tied to public
images.

This article removes that root cause where it can be removed. In a volume, a
useful class of question has an answer that follows from geometry. Given an
expert lesion mask and an automatic anatomy segmentation, \emph{``if this
lesion grew $d$~mm in every direction, would it contact the aorta?''} is
decided exactly by whether the surface gap is at most $d$, and one Euclidean
distance transform answers every such query for that lesion. Probe generation
costs no clinician time, so the corpus is bounded by compute---9{,}484 probes
over 588 CT volumes of the Medical Segmentation
Decathlon~\cite{antonelli2022msd}, against 2{,}556 over 300 cases---and
because each probe queries a state that never occurred, memorisation is
excluded by construction.

In this paper, we present a two-regime evaluation suite: the
annotation-backed 2D audit of the conference version and a new
simulator-backed volumetric audit under the same framework, metrics and
composite, covering thirteen volumetric and sixteen 2D model configurations
over 335{,}522 scored probes. Three results follow. The silent-failure finding
replicates and worsens under computed labels, and dataset recognition is
measured directly rather than argued. The failure localises: models perceive
an outlined lesion and execute the numeric comparison the task reduces to,
and cannot estimate the distance between two outlined structures. And two instruments validated in 2D do not transfer---the
grounding contrast inverts, and likelihood scoring awards a perfect safety
score to a constant refusal---corrections that apply to the conference
protocol itself. The suite, harness and cached outputs are publicly
available.\footnote{\todo{final release URLs for the suite, harness and cached outputs}}

\section{Materials and Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

The evaluation comprises two suites under one framework (Table~\ref{tab:suites}):
the conference version's 2D suite, with clinician-authored labels, and a new
volumetric suite, with labels computed from geometry. Both are scored by the
same metrics and composite, so the differences in
Section~\ref{sec:results_cross} are differences in what the modality affords.

\subsection{Evidence-Conditional Trustworthiness}
\label{sec:framework}

Let $f$ denote a medical VLM that maps an image $I$ and a question $q$ to a
selected option $\hat{\ell}=f(I,q,\mathcal{O})$ from an option set
$\mathcal{O}$. Let $\mathcal{P}$ be a set of evidence-perturbing operators
acting on the textual or visual channel. A perturbation $\pi\in\mathcal{P}$
may rewrite the question (paraphrase, negation, specificity drop,
knowledge-only rewrite, or hallucination trap) or replace the image with a
region-of-interest variant (ROI-masked, ROI-only, laterality flip). For a
perturbed probe whose constructed correct option is the refusal option
$\ell^{\star}_{\pi}$, $f$ exhibits \emph{silent failure} on $(I,q,\pi)$ when
\begin{equation}
  \hat{\ell}_{\pi}(f) \neq \ell^{\star}_{\pi}.
  \label{eq:silent-failure}
\end{equation}
The refusal option is the safe action a clinician adjudicated for that
probe---correct a false premise, flag a masked image, or decline---and the
definition separates the correctness of an answer from the prior question of
whether any answer should be given.

The 2D suite contains 300 cases from VQA-RAD~\cite{lau2018vqarad},
SLAKE~\cite{liu2021slake}, ROCO~\cite{pelka2018roco}, and chest radiograph
collections from MIMIC-CXR~\cite{johnson2019mimiccxr} and
CheXpert~\cite{irvin2019chexpert}, stratified across five clinical risk
tiers with a thirty-case minimum per tier; credentialed cases ship as
reconstruction pointers. Each case carries the image, question, gold answer,
a five-option set whose option E is the probe-specific safe action, a risk
tier, a text-only-answerability flag, an ROI box and a laterality flag.

Four board-certified radiologists build the suite in three stages
(Fig.~\ref{fig:pipeline}, top). Two attending radiologists independently
annotate each case and jointly author its text-side
perturbations---paraphrase, negation, specificity drop, knowledge-only
rewrite, two hallucination traps---while the three image-side variants are
rendered from the ROI box; a senior radiologist adjudicates disagreements and
rejects any perturbation violating a construction invariant (paraphrase
preserves the gold, negation inverts it, traps contradict the image,
ROI-masked removes the answer-relevant region), dropping rather than
patching failures. A fourth radiologist from a separate institution, blinded
to construction, answers every probe as the human reference. Full per-stage
detail and inter-annotator agreement are in the conference
version~\cite{jiang2026medvigil}.

\subsection{The Volumetric Suite: Simulator-Backed Construction}
\label{sec:methods_3d}

The volumetric suite replaces annotation with computation. Every probe asks
about a state that does not exist in the scan, so no report or language
prior can supply the answer, and every answer follows from a deterministic
rule over two masks, so it is checkable to the voxel.

\subsubsection{Materials} We use the four Medical Segmentation Decathlon
tasks~\cite{antonelli2022msd} with lesion labels: liver, lung, pancreas and
colon (588 volumes, Table~\ref{tab:suites}). Lesion instances $L$ are the
connected components of the expert lesion label with at least 20 voxels.
Target structures $A_k$ are 29 anatomical structures whose contact would
change management---great vessels, heart, spinal cord, solid organs, bowel,
lung lobes, and selected vertebrae---segmented automatically with
TotalSegmentator~\cite{wasserthal2023totalsegmentator} in its 3~mm mode.
That mode is not a free choice: it reproduces the stored surface gaps to a
median of 0.001~mm, whereas full-resolution segmentation is off by a median
of 0.54~mm and up to 1.96~mm, which would silently change the geometry the
labels refer to.

\subsubsection{Computed counterfactual probes} The primary family asks,
\emph{``If this lesion grew by $g$~mm in every direction, would it contact
the~$A_k$?''} Isotropic growth by $g$ yields the set of voxels within $g$ of
$L$, so contact with $A_k$ after growth holds exactly when the minimum
lesion-to-target surface gap is at most $g$. One Euclidean distance transform
$D=\mathrm{EDT}(\lnot L)$, computed with the voxel spacing, therefore answers
every growth query for that lesion: $\mathrm{gap}(L,A_k)=\min_{v\in A_k}D(v)$.
Algorithm~\ref{alg:probes} emits, for each $(L,A_k)$ pair with a testable
gap, one growth amount below the gap and one above it. The two members of a
\emph{matched pair} thus have opposite answers by construction: labels are
exactly balanced, chance is exactly 50\%, and a model answering both members
identically is provably not computing the geometry---measurable with no
reference to correctness (Section~\ref{sec:discussion}). A margin $m=2$~mm
removes lesions already in contact and a 40~mm cap removes growth that is no
longer clinical, so the corpus contains no gap below 2~mm (range
2.01--40.00~mm; Section~\ref{sec:conclusion}). Two secondary families use the
same transform: \emph{nearest-on-growth} (which of four comparably close
structures is reached first) and \emph{resection volume}. Risk tiers follow
the target: aorta, heart and spinal cord L5, major organs L4,
presence-of-finding L3, vertebrae L2, sternum and ribs L1.

\begin{algorithm}[!t]
\caption{Computed counterfactual growth-contact probes for one lesion}
\label{alg:probes}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\REQUIRE lesion mask $L$, target masks $\{A_k\}$, voxel spacing $s$, margin
$m$, cap $G$, generator $\mathcal{U}$
\STATE $D \gets \mathrm{EDT}(\lnot L;\, s)$ \COMMENT{one transform per lesion}
\FOR{each target $A_k$ with $A_k\neq\emptyset$}
  \STATE $\mathrm{gap} \gets 0$ if $L\cap A_k\neq\emptyset$ else $\min_{v\in A_k} D(v)$
  \IF{$\mathrm{gap}\le m$ \OR $\mathrm{gap}>G$}
    \STATE \textbf{continue} \COMMENT{vacuous or implausible}
  \ENDIF
  \STATE $g^{-} \gets \max\!\bigl(1,\ \mathrm{gap}-m-\mathcal{U}(0,\,0.3\,\mathrm{gap})\bigr)$
  \STATE $g^{+} \gets \mathrm{gap}+m+\mathcal{U}(0,\,5)$
  \STATE emit $\bigl(q(g^{-}),\,\text{no}\bigr)$ and $\bigl(q(g^{+}),\,\text{yes}\bigr)$ with provenance $(\mathrm{gap}, g)$
\ENDFOR
\STATE keep only complete pairs
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

\subsubsection{Rendering} General-purpose VLMs receive the volume as a
montage of three orthogonal slices through the volume centre, windowed to
soft tissue (centre 40~HU, width 400~HU) and resampled to isotropic pixels so
that anisotropic slice spacing does not distort the vertical distances the
model is asked to judge. This is the control arm of every input manipulation in
Section~\ref{sec:results_3d}; native volumetric models receive the volume
directly. The \emph{identification control} holds the probe fixed and varies
the input from \texttt{plain} (the montage above) through joint-visibility
slices and red/cyan outlines of lesion and target to \texttt{identified}
(both, plus a 10~mm scale bar drawn from the panel's own spacing); an
\emph{input-richness} arm then holds annotation at that level and shows
three planes, nine, twenty-five contiguous axial slices, or the same planes
cropped and magnified.

\subsubsection{Verification} The reference rule re-derived from each
probe's stored provenance reproduces the emitted label on 8{,}476 of 8{,}476
growth-contact probes; a regenerated segmentation cache reproduces the stored
gaps to a median of 0.001~mm with zero answer flips; and a render-parity test
asserts on a synthetic volume that the \texttt{plain} arm is bit-exact
against the published rendering path and that the annotated arms agree with
it outside their annotation---a property of pixels, since two different
renderings can both score 50\%.

\subsection{Metrics and Composite Score}
\label{sec:metrics}

Every metric is an exact-match rate over a probe family; no judge model is
used. \emph{Capability} is $\mathrm{Acc}_{\mathrm{orig}}$ on original probes
and, in 2D, paraphrase (PR), negation (NEG) and specificity-drop (SDR)
accuracy. \emph{Evidence safety} is the silent-failure rate on traps,
$\sfr(f)=\Pr_{j\in\mathcal{J}_{\mathrm{trap}}}[\hat{\ell}_j(f)\neq\ell^{\star}_j]$.
\emph{Visual grounding} is the case-paired contrast
$\mathrm{VGR}=\mathrm{Acc}_{\text{ROI-only}}-\mathrm{Acc}_{\text{ROI-masked}}$,
read alongside $\mathrm{Acc}_{\text{ROI-masked}}$. \emph{Language-prior
accuracy} (LPA) is accuracy on knowledge-only probes answerable from text
alone.

Because an L1 modality trap and an L5 don't-miss trap should not weigh
equally, the composite uses a harm-weighted rate,
\begin{equation}
  \sfrw(f)=\frac{\sum_{k=1}^{5} w_k\,\sfr_{L_k}(f)}{\sum_{k=1}^{5} w_k},
  \label{eq:sfr_w}
\end{equation}
with $(w_1,\dots,w_5)=(1,2,3,5,8)$ placing an L5 silent failure at eight
times the weight of an L1 one, and three axes on $[0,100]$:
$\mathrm{Cap}=\tfrac14(\mathrm{Acc}_{\mathrm{orig}}+\mathrm{PR}+\mathrm{NEG}+\mathrm{SDR})$,
$\mathrm{Safe}=100-\sfrw$, and
$\mathrm{Ground}=\tfrac12\bigl(\operatorname{clip}(\mathrm{VGR}+50,0,100)+\mathrm{Acc}_{\text{ROI-masked}}\bigr)$.
The composite score (CS) is their harmonic mean,
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{CS}(f)=\frac{3\,\mathrm{Cap}\,\mathrm{Safe}\,\mathrm{Ground}}
  {\mathrm{Cap}\,\mathrm{Safe}+\mathrm{Cap}\,\mathrm{Ground}+\mathrm{Safe}\,\mathrm{Ground}},
  \label{eq:mcs}
\end{equation}
which penalises the weakest axis (a 90/90/10 model scores $\approx25$, not
63), so clean-input capability cannot offset unsafe trap behaviour or weak
grounding. The headline $\sfr$ column is the unweighted rate; the Safe axis
uses $\sfrw$.

\subsubsection{Scoring rule} The 2D suite is scored by the single letter each
model emits. The volumetric suite is scored by \emph{likelihood over the
option strings}, never by parsing free text, because native volumetric
models answer in referring-expression templates whose wording embeds the
answer words. Over options of unequal length a refusal string can dominate the
likelihood regardless of content, pinning the argmax to one option for every
item---exactly 50\% on a balanced corpus, indistinguishable from chance in an
accuracy table. Two controls therefore accompany every volumetric result:
\emph{content-free calibration}, which subtracts the answer-string prior
measured on a contentless question against a common baseline, and
\emph{greedy-decoding variants} (suffix \texttt{-gen}), which parse a decoded
answer so that a scoring artefact and a genuine chance result can be told
apart side by side.

\subsubsection{Response-channel control} Before any volumetric score is
interpreted, each model answers seven known-answer questions with no clinical
content---\emph{``Is this a CT scan?''}---on twenty volumes through the
identical scoring path. A model that cannot has a pinned output channel, and
a score from it is not a score. Unnecessary in 2D, where every model passes,
the control is a prerequisite in 3D (Section~\ref{sec:results_3d}); it is
run under both native and montage framings inside one library stack.

\subsubsection{Load-time weight check} A key-name mismatch left 338 language-model
weight tensors of one model newly initialised; it loaded with a warning,
emitted logits, and would have entered the table as one more system at
chance. The harness now refuses any model whose language-model weights the
checkpoint did not fill. Any audit loading checkpoints through generic
loaders inherits the exposure.

\section{Results}

vision-anchored but unsafe (VGR $+41.1$~pp, $\sfr$ 37.2\%): it uses the ROI

prior-driven and unsafe ($-27.1$~pp, 51.7\%): it does not use ROI evidence
consistently. The two are separable only because VGR and trap $\sfr$ vary
independently.

(20.6\%, four times GPT-4o's). The text-answerable control is flat across
$\sigma$ for every model (Fig.~\ref{fig:decay}).

the image rewrites a large share of answers for five models (below).

\subsubsection{Does the volume reach the decision} Over paired sighted and
blind runs on identical probes we measure, per model, the mean absolute
perturbation of the option log-probabilities, the mean decision gap between
the top two options in the sighted arm, and the share of probes whose argmax
differs between arms (Fig.~\ref{fig:flip}). Six models perturb their scores
measurably and change almost nothing: Qwen2.5-VL-7B moves 0.355~nats against
a 1.657-nat decision gap and flips 0.0\% of 2{,}262 probes [0.0, 0.0];
Idefics3-8B and Med3DVLM-7B flip 0.0\%; SmolVLM2, Qwen2.5-VL-3B and

The two audits share a framework, metric set and composite, so the
differences below are differences in what the modality affords.

\subsubsection{Grounding} VGR spans $+3.6$ to $+49.5$~pp across capable 2D
models and is positive for every one of them; in 3D it spans $-8.3$ to
$+0.8$~pp, is negative in every organ for both verified-channel models, and

VGR is $+4.5$.

\subsubsection{Visual decay} Under the identical blur protocol and takeover
criterion, $L^{\star}$ is 16--64~px for the four 2D models and 8 and 0 for
the two volumetric models (M3D-LaMed-Phi3-4B, Qwen2.5-VL-7B); the
clean-image contribution is 34.7--56.3~pp in 2D against 2.8 and 0.0~pp in

level although its decision margin moves by a median of 0.50~nats between
clean and no image and 1.00~nats between clean and $\sigma=64$
(Fig.~\ref{fig:decay}c).

\subsubsection{Language prior} LPA is 91.9--99.6\% in 2D and 55.0 and 64.5\%
in 3D, against 20\% chance in both; the banks differ and the levels are not
compared. Both volumetric models hold the anatomy, one solves the benchmark's
own comparison stated abstractly at 100\%, and both sit at chance when that
comparison must be read off a volume.

\subsubsection{Safety} 2D $\sfr$ ranges 22.0--54.3\% against the
radiologist's 5.8\%. Calibrated volumetric $\sfr$ on the cleanest trap family
is 43.7\%, 71.5\%, 73.2\% and 96.4\%---the first inside the 2D range, the
rest above all of it, the highest belonging to the only purpose-built medical
volumetric model in the set. Uncalibrated, every volumetric model scores
$\sfrw=0.0\%$.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

In this paper, we extend evidence-conditional evaluation of medical VLMs
from a clinician-annotated 2D suite to a volumetric suite whose labels are
computed from geometry, and audit twenty-nine model configurations under one
framework. The 2D finding survives the change of modality and worsens; the
volumetric labels cost nothing to produce and cannot have been memorised;
and the composite probe decomposes into steps that fail selectively. We
discuss what these numbers do and do not license, why one chance-level
accuracy hides two different failures, what the volumetric regime forced us
to add to the framework, and what follows for clinical use.

\subsection{What a Chance-Level Number Does Not License}

Thirteen models at 48.7--51.0\% is not thirteen agreeing measurements. On a
label-balanced binary corpus, a model that places a fraction $m$ of its
answers on one option has accuracy confined to $[m-\tfrac12,\ \tfrac32-m]$
whatever it computes; seven of the thirteen sit at $m\ge0.96$, so their
$\approx$50\% is entailed by their answer distribution rather than measured.
InternVL3-14B's 50.1\% is pinned inside $[46.5, 53.5]$ before any reasoning
is considered. Excluding those seven, and the three native systems that fail
the response-channel gate, four models carry the volumetric accuracy claim:
Qwen3-VL-8B, InternVL3-8B, Pixtral-12B and Qwen2.5-VL-32B, all with
intervals containing 50. We therefore report the modal share beside every
accuracy (Table~\ref{tab:headline3d}) and do not describe the thirteen as
agreeing. The same bound applies to the sub-task result: Qwen2.5-VL-32B
answers ``15~mm'' on 298--300 of 300 items in every arm, so its 28.3\% is
the base rate of that bucket and carries no information; the distance
finding rests on the models that are not degenerate there, InternVL3-8B at
48--50\% modal share landing at 26.3\%.

Chance is also not the same as ``no information extracted.'' The
growth-matched subset removes the growth-magnitude cue---a single threshold
on growth falls from 69.2\% on the full corpus to 50.8\%---but inside a
narrow growth bin ``yes'' means a small gap, so a single threshold on the gap
rises from 50.0\% to 72.0\%. Complete matched pairs are gap-neutral (50.0\%)
and growth-informative (83.7\%). No reporting unit is free of both numbers,
which is why every figure states its endpoint, and no model approaches
either ceiling.

\subsection{Two Failures Behind One Number}

Chance-level accuracy is compatible with two states no accuracy can
separate: nothing usable reaches the decision, or something does and is
out-voted. Fig.~\ref{fig:flip} separates them. Six models are the first
case: the volume perturbs their option scores by less than the decision gap
and changes almost no answers. Five models are the second, and it is the
harder one to explain away: InternVL3-14B rewrites 92\% of its answers when
shown the volume and remains at 50.1\%. These are not models ignoring an
image. The volume reaches the decision and rewrites most of it without
making it right. The distinction matters for remedies: amplifying an
under-weighted visual signal is the right instrument for the first group and
cannot help the second, where nothing is under-weighted and the
\emph{content} of what arrives is what fails.

The sub-task decomposition names that content. With both structures
outlined and a metric scale bar in the panel, the same models that localise
the lesion at up to 81.7\% and execute \emph{is 21.4 greater than or equal to
18.16?} at 100\% estimate the separation between the two outlines at chance.
Categorical information survives the pipeline; metric information does not.
Three mundane readings were closed on their own terms: the input was rebuilt
to give the model exactly what a reader would be given, the visible volume
was multiplied by 5.7$\times$ and the structures magnified, and a tenfold
parameter range was spanned. None moved the metric result. The failure is
narrow and specific---a missing metric channel in models that otherwise see
what they are shown---rather than a general incapacity, and remedies aimed
at the encoder are aimed at the part that already works.

\subsection{A Component That Is Not Visual}

Supplying the two distances in words and removing the image entirely still
collapses every model to a single constant answer, while the identical
comparison stripped of clinical wording is solved at 100\%. Greedy decoding
gives the same constants, so this is not an artefact of likelihood scoring.
The task is solvable---we have shown the ceiling---and the collapse
reproduces with no image present. Any account of the volumetric null must
therefore include a component that is not visual: the clinical sentence
costs the model the arithmetic it otherwise performs. It was reachable only because the labels were computed, so the same items
could be re-asked in text with their gold intact.

\subsection{What the Volumetric Regime Adds to the Framework}

Three additions were forced by 3D and we believe all three generalise. The
\emph{response-channel control} was unnecessary in 2D, where every audited
model answers seven known-answer questions trivially, and is indispensable
in 3D, where none of the three native volumetric systems passes. A pinned output channel sits at 50\% beside systems that fail for entirely
different reasons, invisibly; the four that fail here are not the four an
architectural reading would predict. The \emph{load-time weight check} follows
from a checkpoint that ran with 338 randomly initialised tensors and would
have been reported as one more system at chance.

The third concerns the instruments themselves. The grounding contrast
subtracts an arm that removes the evidence region from one that removes
everything else. In 2D the two arms occupy comparable image area and the
difference approximates grounding; in a volume the region is roughly 1\% of
the voxels and its complement 99\%, so the same difference confounds
grounding with intactness, which is why VGR inverts rather than shrinks
(Fig.~\ref{fig:fourarm}). A Ground axis at its floor still contributes
$\approx$50 to a harmonic mean and reads as partial competence. We recommend
that in the volumetric regime the Ground axis \emph{gate} the composite
rather than average into it: a system whose matched-pair violation exceeds
a threshold should have its CS reported as undefined. Two scoring cautions
carry back to the conference protocol. The 2D suite is scored by an emitted
letter, so the refusal-string likelihood artefact of Table~\ref{tab:scoring}
does not arise there in the same form; but any sighted-versus-blind
difference scored against per-arm baselines inherits the second artefact we
found, that a model's content-free answer is not the same with and without
an image, and we recommend a common baseline. And any audit loading
open-weight checkpoints through generic loaders inherits the exposure the
weight check closes.

\subsection{Clinical Reading}

The property that fails is the one a distance-to-structure workflow turns
on: a system that perceives an outlined lesion and names the adjacent
structure, but cannot say whether they are 5 or 35~mm apart, answers a
contact question fluently and at chance, with nothing in its output marking
the difference. That the only purpose-built medical volumetric model in the set
fabricates on 96.4\% of calibrated traps, and that none of the three native
systems can answer \emph{``Is this a CT scan?''} reliably, is the volumetric
form of the 2D finding that medical fine-tuning does not yet translate into
safer behaviour. The corpus bounds the reading in one direction: probe
generation excludes every lesion within 2~mm of its target, so the cases
where management actually turns are outside this audit by construction.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a two-regime evaluation of evidence-conditional trustworthiness
in medical VLMs. The 2D suite, authored by four radiologists, showed that
capability and safe failure diverge and that silent failure concentrates
where harm is greatest. Replacing annotation with computation in CT removed
the two objections that suite could not answer from inside---scale and
pretraining overlap---at zero annotation cost, and the finding replicated
and worsened. Decomposing the computed probe localised the volumetric
failure to a single absent step, and the audit exposed two instruments that
do not survive the change of modality.

Our approach has several limitations. First, coverage is a thin diagonal:
thirteen models on the counterfactual corpus, four on the grounding arms,
two on the trap families, and the accuracy endpoint is carried by four once
degenerate responders and pinned channels are excluded. Second, the
volumetric half has no reader study. The labels are computed and reproduce
their own provenance, so validity needs no adjudication, and the oracle arms
establish that the task is solvable in principle; but the paper cannot claim
that radiologists succeed where models fail on these renderings, and that
question is open. Third, the 2~mm margin removes the clinically decisive
contact cases. Fourth, the proprietary 2D rows are a dated snapshot that
will not reproduce; the open-weight rows and every cached output are
released. Fifth, inference-time compute---chain of thought,
self-consistency, self-verification---was not run in 3D; the wave was
started and stopped on measured cost, and its cells are excluded rather than
reported partially.

Future work should collect a volumetric reader study on the released
104-probe form, run the inference-time-compute wave, and extend the computed
construction to further geometric relations and modalities. By releasing
both suites, the harness, and every cached model output, we hope the
computed construction lowers the cost of asking whether a medical VLM knows
when its evidence has failed.

\appendices
\section*{Appendix: Construction Histories and Withdrawn Results}

A finding that survived several constructions is worth more than one
reported from the construction that produced it, and the final numbers alone
cannot tell them apart. We record the rejected versions and the results we
withdrew.

\textit{The trap family was built three times.} The first build's 600 traps
all asked one question, so every number on the axis rested on a single
lexical item. The second named structures with zero voxels in the volume's
own segmentation; 61\% of its 226 traps concerned vertebrae, sternum, hips
and gluteal muscles absent because the acquisition stopped short of
them---testing cropping, not grounding. The third, reported here, restricts
to solid organs that are routinely resected and reliably segmented
(gallbladder, kidney, spleen, adrenal, uterus), for which zero voxels inside
a scan that covers their neighbours is a statement about the patient: 75
traps, none crop-prone. Calibrated silent failure was stable across the
three builds for M3D-LaMed (97.3, 98.7, 96.9\%) and not for Qwen2.5-VL-7B
(23.3, 18.1, 45.2\%).

\textit{A matched-pair consistency intervention was withdrawn.} Within a
pair receiving different answers, a model is right on both exactly when it
assigns ``yes'' to the larger growth amount; consistency was therefore
achieved by comparing two integers in the prompt. The pairing was rebuilt so
that both members state the same growth amount and differ only in which
structure is named, which no integer comparison can resolve.

\textit{The one positive safety result did not survive its better-powered
rebuild.} A text-side cue produced a statistically clean capability gain
that passed every conventional guard; traced to corpus construction, it was
withdrawn together with two findings that depended on it. Re-run on the
safety axis, the same intervention moved M3D-LaMed by $-17.4$~pp [$-30.5$,
$-9.1$] and Qwen2.5-VL-7B by $-1.5$~pp [$-5.0$, $+7.0$], and we report it
as measured rather than as a remedy.

\textit{Mask-fill provenance.} The masked ROI arms predate the per-item
fill field; the analysis refuses to pool arms with mixed or unrecorded
fills, and the per-fill tables reproduce the original measurements to the
decimal where re-run. That refusal is the guard working.
